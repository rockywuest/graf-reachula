Count Reachula

Spooky Reachy · a friendly Halloween ghost for your Reachy Mini

He lurks perfectly still, jumps up with a “BOO!” when someone walks by, fills the room with creaking doors and howling wind, and plays dramatic ghostly moves. Give him an AI key and he talks: he guesses costumes through his camera, reads spooky futures and tells three-sentence ghost stories.

Spooky, but child-friendly: a startle now and then, never gore. English & Deutsch.

👻

Jump scares

Lurks head-down, then BOO! when a guest steps in front of the camera.

🔊

Spooky sounds

Heartbeat, ghost, creaking door, wind, bells, laughter.

💃

Spooky moves

Trembling, a dramatic ghostly faint, a wobbly zap, a monster roar.

🗣️

Talking Count

Bring your own AI: Claude, OpenAI, xAI Grok, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, Ollama or any compatible API.

Install

  1. Open Reachy Mini Control → Applications → Discover Apps.
  2. Install Count Reachula and start it.
  3. Open the app page to choose English or Deutsch, test the effects and (optionally) add keys.

Tip: put him next to the candy bowl. 🍬 Talking mode hears the room and uses the camera: read the privacy note in the README first.

🇩🇪 Graf Reachula
